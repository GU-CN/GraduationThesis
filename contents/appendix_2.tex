\section*{Purpose}

This appendix collects manufacturer reference parameters, component-level construction details, engineering drawings, and the complete parts record. These items support procurement, maintenance, replication, and hardware verification, but they are separated from Chapter~4 so that the main engineering analysis remains focused on design suitability, workspace, contact tolerance, sensing coverage, learning interfaces, and system operation.

\section*{Franka Research 3 reference parameters}

\section*{Compliant gripper drawing and fabrication reference}

Each gripper assembly uses two fingers of the same geometry. The left gripper has two TPU~95A fingers, while the right gripper has two TPU~64D fingers, giving four fingers in the dual-arm system. One finger has an overall bounding box of \(123.36\times25.80\times40.00~\mathrm{mm}\), corresponding to \(L/T\approx4.78\) and \(L/H\approx3.08\). It is screw mounted, with a reinforced attachment region and a tapered ribbed body.
