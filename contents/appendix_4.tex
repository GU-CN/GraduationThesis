\section*{Detailed use description}

\noindent\textbf{Reference verification and formatting (E1).}
GPT-5.6 Sol assisted with checking the completeness and consistency of the
reference list and with identifying possible GB/T 7714--2015 formatting
problems. No suggested reference was accepted solely on the basis of an AI
response. Bibliographic records and the claims supported by them were checked
against the corresponding source materials by the authors.

\noindent\textbf{Translation and language polishing (D3).}
GPT-5.6 Sol assisted with English translation, expression, grammar, and
readability. The authors retained control of the technical meaning and checked
domain-specific terms concerning bimanual manipulation, vision-language-action
models, imitation learning, ROS~2, and LeRobot. Hardware names, including the
Intel RealSense D435i and D405, as well as equations, parameters, results, and
conclusions, were checked against the project records before the edits were
accepted.

\noindent\textbf{Code writing and debugging (C2).}
GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 assisted with selected programming and debugging
tasks associated with ROS~2 data collection, multi-camera packet handling,
LeRobot dataset and training interfaces, policy inference, and robot-interface
integration. The authors selected, revised, integrated, and executed the final
code. Model suggestions were treated as implementation proposals rather than
as evidence that a function was correct.

\section*{Academic integrity declaration}

The AI tools described above were used only for auxiliary work. They did not
independently determine the research design, generate experimental data, select
the final engineering solution, evaluate task success, or determine the
conclusions of this thesis. All experimental records, numerical results,
figures, tables, and conclusions were checked against the executed software and
the project records. The authors made all final decisions and retain full
academic responsibility for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and content
of the thesis.
