\begin{abstract}[zh]
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘 \quad 要}
现代 3C 电子装配线需要处理小型元器件、多品种生产和接触密集型操作，而传统固定程序机器人仍难以灵活完成这类任务。本项目针对这一缺口，面向双臂工业操作开发视觉-语言-动作（Vision-Language-Action, VLA）系统。系统运行于双臂 Franka 平台，并完成了三个代表性任务：将带手柄的圆环套放到圆柱罐上、将两脚插头插入两孔插座，以及从主板上取下内存条并放入黄色托盘。
\par
最终部署系统采用在三个任务上联合训练的统一 $\pi_{0.5}$ 检查点，接收数据包关联的多视角观测、16 维双臂机器人状态和自然语言任务指令，并在闭环执行过程中输出双臂及夹爪动作。三个任务均由同一个检查点执行，并分别输入对应的任务指令。结果表明，一个统一检查点可以在双臂平台上执行多项任务。
\par
最终实机验证对每项任务进行了 25 次试验，共计 75 次。圆环任务、插头插入任务和内存条任务的成功率分别为 84\%、52\% 和 100\%，总体成功率为 78.7\%。规划、元件检测、失败检测和自动恢复分别采用独立的 25 次实验，其中失败检测与自动恢复使用两组不同的实验，所得成功率分别为 92\%、96\%、92\% 和 92\%。全部 59 次成功完成的任务均满足 ES4 的 20~mm 位置误差要求。六项工程指标均达到项目规定的阈值，但插头插入任务未能单独达到 70\% 的任务执行目标，表明精细对准与插入仍是当前系统的主要改进方向。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}
Modern 3C electronics assembly lines deal with small components, high product variety, and contact-rich operations that remain difficult to automate with conventional fixed-program robots. This project targets this gap by developing a Vision-Language-Action (VLA) system for bimanual industrial manipulation. The system runs on a dual-arm Franka platform and performs three representative tasks: placing a handled ring over a can, inserting a two-pin plug into a two-hole socket, and removing a memory module from a motherboard and placing it in a yellow tray.
\par
The final deployed system uses a unified $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoint trained jointly across the three tasks. It receives packet-associated multi-view observations, a 16-dimensional bilateral robot state, and a natural-language task instruction, and outputs bimanual robot and gripper actions during closed-loop execution. All three tasks were executed by the same checkpoint with their corresponding task instructions, demonstrating unified multi-task bimanual deployment on the physical workcell.
\par
The final physical validation comprised 25 trials per task and 75 trials in total. The ring, plug-insertion, and memory-module tasks achieved success rates of 84\%, 52\%, and 100\%, respectively, producing an overall success rate of 78.7\%. Planning, component detection, failure detection, and automatic recovery used independent 25-experiment test sets; failure detection and recovery were evaluated on two different groups. Their observed rates were 92\%, 96\%, 92\%, and 92\%, respectively. All 59 completed tasks satisfied the ES4 positional tolerance of 20~mm. All six project-level metrics met their stated thresholds, although plug insertion did not independently meet the 70\% execution target and remains the main area for improvement.
\end{abstract}
